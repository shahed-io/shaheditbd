**Signature SHAHED STORE AI Card Design (NON-NEGOTIABLE):**

Reference cards (gold standard): Hix.AI, Duolingo, Crunchyroll Premium, ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro, AutoDesk — uploaded by user as the new house style.

**Background:** Soft pastel MESH gradient — lavender (top-left, #c9b8e8) → pink (top-right, #f4b8c8) → sky blue (bottom-left, #bcd0ec) → peach (bottom-right, #f5c8b0). Smooth diagonal blend, dreamy daylight feel.

**Bubbles:** 6–12 CLEAR transparent soap bubbles (no color tint). Each: thin white outline ring + crescent highlight upper-left + bright reflection dot. Sizes vary 40–220px, scattered around card edges, some overlapping the card for depth.

**Main Card:** Centered ~80% ultra-transparent frosted glass (rgba(255,255,255,0.18)), 32px radius, thin 1.5px white border, soft shadow. Pastel gradient clearly shows through.

**TOP-LEFT pill (CRITICAL — NEW):** A TRANSLUCENT GRAY-GLASS pill (rgba(180,180,200,0.35), backdrop blur, thin white inner border) with "SHAHED STORE" in WHITE BOLD uppercase directly on the glass. NO red badge anymore — just clean glass pill with white text.

**TOP-RIGHT pill:** Solid white pill (rgba(255,255,255,0.98)) with brand logo (left, ~34px, original colors) + brand/product name in dark charcoal (#1f2937) bold sans-serif.

**Center hero:** Very large square app icon or official brand logo (~50–60% card width), vertically centered, soft drop shadow. Brand wordmark may appear under icon ONLY if part of official lockup (Duolingo, Crunchyroll style).

**Bottom contact:** Single contact row near bottom (~32px from bottom), INSIDE the card, NO separator line above:
- Left: outlined globe icon (with cursor arrow) + `www.shahedstore.com.bd`
- Right: outlined phone icon + `+880 1840-099853`
Text in dark charcoal, ~16px bold.

**Style variants only override** the canvas background tone and bubble accent — the SHAHED STORE glass pill, brand white pill, contact line, and overall layout MUST remain identical across all 6 styles (Dark Neon, Light Glass, Clean Light, Soft Aurora, Glass Gradient, Glassmorphism UI).

**OLD design (DEPRECATED — do not use):** pale-blue/white background with sky-blue + mint bubbles and WHITE pill containing RED SHAHED STORE badge. Replaced fully by the new pastel mesh design above.
