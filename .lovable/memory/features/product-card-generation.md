**Signature SHAHED STORE AI Card Design (NON-NEGOTIABLE):**

**Background:** Soft pale-blue / off-white airy gradient (#e8eef5 → #c8d6e8). NO pink, peach, lavender, purple, or yellow — ONLY pale blue / white tones.

**Bubbles:** 6–10 soft 3D translucent glossy spheres in ONLY sky-blue (#a8c5e8) and mint/teal (#7dd3c8). Other colors are forbidden. Some blurred for depth, scattered around card edges.

**Main Card:** Centered ~82% white frosted glass (rgba(255,255,255,0.5)), 28px radius, thin white border (rgba(255,255,255,0.85)), soft drop shadow.

**TOP-LEFT pill (CRITICAL):** A WHITE outer pill containing an INNER RED rectangle badge (#e63946) with "SHAHED STORE" in WHITE bold uppercase. The outer container is white; the inner SHAHED STORE label is on a RED background. NEVER a glass pill with white text.

**TOP-RIGHT pill:** Solid white pill with brand logo (left, ~34px) + brand/product name in dark charcoal (#1f2937) bold sans-serif.

**Center hero:** Very large square app icon or official brand logo (~50–60% card width), vertically centered, soft drop shadow.

**Bottom contact:** Thin separator line (1px, rgba(0,0,0,0.08)), then a contact row INSIDE the card:
- Left: outlined globe icon (with cursor arrow) + `www.shahedstore.com.bd`
- Right: outlined phone icon + `+880 1840-099853`
Text in dark charcoal, ~15–16px bold.

**Style variants only override** the canvas background tone and bubble accent — the SHAHED STORE pill, brand pill, contact line, and overall layout MUST remain identical across all 6 styles (Dark Neon, Light Glass, Clean Light, Soft Aurora, Glass Gradient, Glassmorphism UI).

**Reference cards** (gold standard): LinkedIn, Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma Premium, Microsoft Office 365, Windows 11 Pro — all stored in `product-images` bucket with `ai-card-*` prefix.
